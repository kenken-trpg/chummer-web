Third-party data and translations
---------------------------------

1. Game data and base translations — chummer5a/chummer5a (GPL-3.0)
   https://github.com/chummer5a/chummer5a

   Chummer/data/*.xml and Chummer/lang/{en-us,ja-jp,ja-jp_data}.xml are
   downloaded at build time by backend/scripts/fetch_chummer_data.py into
   backend/vendor/ (not tracked in git). This project is a derivative work of
   that dataset and is licensed under the same GPL-3.0.

   ゲームデータと基礎翻訳は chummer5a/chummer5a (GPL-3.0) のもの。ビルド時に
   取得し backend/vendor/ に置く（git 管理外）。本プロジェクトはその派生物で、
   同じく GPL-3.0。

2. Japanese terminology overlay — backend/data/ja_overrides/{data,ui}.json

   A git-tracked overlay applied on top of the vendored ja-jp files. Its
   entries are assembled from:

   a. Hand translations curated for SR5 and checked against a terminology
      glossary (backend/scripts/ja_curated_*.py). The majority of entries.

   b. Exact name / category matches imported from "chumJA" (chumJA_20130129),
      a Shadowrun-4th-edition-era Japanese translation of Chummer. Mostly
      proper-noun katakana and category words. The original distribution
      states no license; the entries are treated as a Chummer derivative
      under GPL-3.0. Per-entry provenance is listed in
      docs/translation-import-report.md.

   c. Terminology cross-checked against — not copied from — the 2021 Chummer
      sheet localisation XSLT ("chummer5th_シート日本語化_52160対応") and
      "shadowrun5eja" (https://github.com/MiyabiRouga/shadowrun5eja), a Foundry
      VTT SR5e Japanese i18n project. Neither ships a LICENSE file; they are
      used for reference only. shadowrun5eja's strings are not reproduced —
      the "sr5eja" column in docs/translation-glossary.md carries only an
      agree/differ marker.

   d. Entry names checked against the official Japanese edition of the
      Shadowrun 5th Edition supplement "Run & Gun" (Run & Gun 日本語版), a
      commercial publication, all rights reserved by its publishers. Only the
      correspondence "English entry name -> the term printed in that edition"
      is recorded, in backend/scripts/ja_curated_rg.py. No rules text, tables,
      headings or artwork from that book is reproduced here, and the overlay
      format cannot hold anything but a name-to-term pair. This tool is not a
      substitute for the book.

   Full methodology: backend/data/ja_overrides/README.md and
   docs/plans/translation-plan.md.

   日本語用語オーバーレイ（backend/data/ja_overrides/）は、SR5 用語集に照らした
   手訳を主とし、一部の固有名カナ・カテゴリ語は chumJA（Chummer の SR4 期日本語訳、
   ライセンス表記なし）由来。エントリ別の出典は docs/translation-import-report.md。
   用語の裏取りに 2021 版シート用語 XSLT と shadowrun5eja（Foundry VTT SR5e
   日本語化、LICENSE なし）を参照している（大量取り込みはしていない）。
   『シャドウラン 5th ラン＆ガン』日本語版（商業出版物・全著作権留保）は、
   項目名の対訳のみを backend/scripts/ja_curated_rg.py に記録している。
   ルール本文・表・見出し・図版は一切収録しておらず、本ツールは書籍の代替
   ではない。

3. Regression fixtures — backend/tests/snapshots/*.json

   Golden-output fixtures for the engine tests. They contain SR5 item names,
   numeric stats and rulebook page numbers derived from the chummer5a dataset
   (GPL-3.0).
